About

Outcome documents, not slide decks.

Each page on this site is a self-contained outcome document, distilling the discussion from one of our weekly classes into a study reference you can return to as you write. These are not slide decks. They are designed to be read independently, in your own time, after the class they correspond to.

New weeks appear here as they are completed. The tabs above let you move between them.
